Quarrystone Energy

Quarrystone Energy develops and operates grid-scale battery storage sites that sell capacity and balancing services to utilities.

About

Quarrystone Energy was founded in 2018 and is headquartered in Calgary, Alberta. Our CEO is Danielle Fournier. We employ about 120 people and operate six sites in Alberta and Texas.

Revenue comes from long-term capacity contracts with utilities and from trading in balancing markets.

Funding

In 2025 Quarrystone closed a C$150 million project financing facility for its next three sites.
