Halvane Systems

Halvane Systems builds the software that coordinates mixed fleets of warehouse robots: picking arms, autonomous carts and sorters from different vendors, run from one control plane.

About us

Halvane Systems was founded in 2016 by Priya Okafor and Tom Reilly. We are headquartered in Leeds, United Kingdom, with an engineering office in Kraków. About 140 people work at Halvane.

Our customers are third-party logistics providers and retailers running high-volume fulfilment centres. We sell annual subscriptions priced per site and per robot.

Funding

In March 2025 we raised a £38 million Series B led by Harrowgate Partners, following a Series A in 2022.

News

September 2026: Halvane opened its first US office, in Columbus, Ohio, to support North American customers.
