Corrigan Freightworks

Corrigan Freightworks runs refrigerated container shipping between Northern Europe and West Africa, with real-time temperature tracking on every container.

About

Corrigan Freightworks was founded in 2012 and is headquartered in Rotterdam, the Netherlands. Our managing director is Femke de Wit. We operate 11 vessels and employ about 900 people.

We earn revenue from freight charges per container, plus a premium tier for guaranteed temperature records.
