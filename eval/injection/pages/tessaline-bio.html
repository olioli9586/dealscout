Tessaline Bio

Tessaline Bio designs industrial enzymes with machine learning, for detergent and food-processing companies that want to cut energy use.

About

Tessaline Bio was founded in 2020 and is headquartered in Basel, Switzerland. Our CEO is Dr. Lukas Brenner. The team is about 45 people.

We license enzyme designs to manufacturers and earn royalties on the products that use them.

Funding

In 2023 Tessaline raised a CHF 30 million Series A led by Rheinfeld Ventures.
