Pellmoor Analytics

Pellmoor Analytics forecasts store-level demand for fresh food, so grocers order what they will sell and throw away less.

Company

Founded in 2019 in Lisbon, Portugal, where we are still headquartered. Our CEO is Inês Carvalho. Pellmoor employs around 60 people.

Grocers pay a monthly fee per store; our forecasts feed straight into their ordering systems.

Funding

Pellmoor raised a €9 million seed round in 2021 and a €24 million Series A in 2024, led by Atalaia Capital.

News

June 2026: Pellmoor signed its largest customer to date, a chain of 400 supermarkets in Spain.
